# Case 5

## Patient Summary
67-year-old female receiving Warfarin.

## Medication
- Warfarin
- Ibuprofen

## Timeline
- Five days after starting Ibuprofen developed black stools and dizziness.

## Assessment

### Symptoms
- Gastrointestinal bleeding
- Dizziness

### Suspected Drug
Ibuprofen (interaction with Warfarin)

### Reason
- Ibuprofen increases bleeding risk.
- Warfarin further increased the risk.
- Symptoms improved after stopping Ibuprofen.

### Conclusion
Serious Adverse Drug Reaction due to a drug interaction.
