# Case 4

## Patient Summary
58-year-old male with Type 2 Diabetes.

## Medication
- Metformin 500 mg twice daily

## Timeline
- Symptoms started after one week.

## Assessment

### Symptoms
- Diarrhea
- Stomach discomfort

### Suspected Drug
Metformin

### Reason
- Common gastrointestinal adverse effects.
- Symptoms improved over time.

### Conclusion
Likely Adverse Drug Reaction caused by Metformin.
