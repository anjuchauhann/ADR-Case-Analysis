# Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Case Analysis

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the analysis of patient case scenarios to identify potential **Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs)**. The objective is to evaluate clinical information by reviewing patient symptoms, medication history, and event timelines to determine whether an adverse event is likely related to a medication and to identify the suspected drug.

The project applies fundamental pharmacovigilance principles used in drug safety monitoring and clinical case assessment.

---

## Objective

* Analyze patient case scenarios.
* Review patient symptoms and clinical history.
* Evaluate medication history, including recently initiated medications.
* Assess the temporal relationship between drug administration and symptom onset.
* Determine whether the adverse event is drug-related.
* Identify the most likely suspected medication responsible for the reaction.
* Document findings using a structured clinical approach.

---

## Methodology

Each patient case was analyzed using the following steps:

1. Review patient demographics and medical history.
2. Identify all medications being taken.
3. Examine the onset and progression of symptoms.
4. Compare symptom onset with medication timelines.
5. Evaluate known adverse effects of the medications.
6. Consider alternative explanations such as underlying diseases or infections.
7. Determine the suspected drug.
8. Assess the likelihood of an ADR based on available clinical evidence.

## Sample Case Analysis

### Case Summary

**Patient:** 28-year-old female

**Medical Condition:** Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)

**Medication:**

* Amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily

**Clinical Timeline:**

* Day 1: Started Amoxicillin
* Day 4: Developed an itchy red rash
* Medication discontinued
* Rash resolved within 3 days

### Assessment

**Observed Reaction**

* Generalized itchy rash

**Suspected Drug**

* Amoxicillin

**Reasoning**

* Symptoms developed after initiation of therapy.
* Skin rash is a known adverse reaction associated with Amoxicillin.
* Symptoms improved following discontinuation of the medication (positive dechallenge).
* No alternative cause was identified.

**Conclusion**
The adverse event is considered a **Probable Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)** associated with Amoxicillin.
