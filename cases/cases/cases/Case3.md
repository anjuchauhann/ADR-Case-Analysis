# Case 3

## Patient Summary
45-year-old female with fever.

## Medication
- Paracetamol
- Azithromycin

## Timeline
- Three days later developed nausea and vomiting.
- Medication continued.
- Symptoms resolved after infection improved.

## Assessment

### Symptoms
- Nausea
- Vomiting

### Suspected Drug
Possibly Azithromycin

### Reason
- Gastrointestinal upset is a known side effect.
- Infection may also explain symptoms.

### Conclusion
Possible ADR, but illness remains an alternative explanation.
