# Case 2

## Patient Summary
62-year-old male with hypertension.

## Medication
- Amlodipine 5 mg once daily

## Timeline
- Started Amlodipine
- After 2 weeks, ankle swelling developed
- Medication changed
- Swelling resolved

## Assessment

### Symptoms
- Bilateral ankle swelling

### Suspected Drug
Amlodipine

### Reason
- Timing matches drug initiation.
- Peripheral edema is a common side effect.
- Swelling improved after changing medication.

### Conclusion
Likely Adverse Drug Reaction caused by Amlodipine.
