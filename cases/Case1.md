# Case 1

## Patient Summary
28-year-old female diagnosed with a urinary tract infection.

## Medication
- Amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily

## Timeline
- Day 1: Started Amoxicillin
- Day 4: Developed itchy red rash
- Medication discontinued
- Rash resolved within 3 days

## Assessment

### Symptoms
- Itchy red rash

### Suspected Drug
Amoxicillin

### Reason
- Rash appeared after starting treatment.
- Rash is a known adverse effect of Amoxicillin.
- Symptoms improved after stopping the medication.

### Conclusion
Probable Adverse Drug Reaction caused by Amoxicillin.
